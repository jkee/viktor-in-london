Viktor in London

methodology · data sources

A personal map for one question: where in London do I want to live? The core signal is my own scored walk notes; everything else is reference data layered around them. All layers cover roughly TfL fare Zones 1–2, filtered to the dotted area on the map plus a 500 m margin.

My notes

Voice memos recorded while walking, transcribed, geocoded and scored from −5 (not for me) to +5 (home) — shown as colored dots. Entirely subjective, deliberately so.

Heat map (liveability)

A computed 0–1 score blending how reachable four things are on foot, with distance decay and diminishing returns (the 2nd nearby café matters far more than the 12th):

score = 0.35·connectivity + 0.30·coffee + 0.20·food + 0.15·good life

Hub stations count triple; ★★★ cafes and 9.0+ restaurants double; Aesop is excluded from good life while full supermarkets weigh extra. Red = amenity desert, green = everything at your doorstep. Full methodology lives in the project's docs/livability.md. Note it measures amenity access only — Soho maxes out precisely because it's Soho; peace and safety are what the walk notes and crime layer are for.

Coffee

Two sources. Independent cafes come from propercoffee.app — only their ★★★ Purist and ★★ Specialty tiers (the "Everyday" tier is skipped). Chain locations (Kiss the Hippo, WatchHouse, Rosslyn, Hagen, Origin, Ozone, Qima) come from each chain's official location pages. Bold-ringed badges are the ★★★ destination-grade places.

Food 8.0+

Restaurants from The Infatuation's London reviews (~2,500 reviews scanned, Aug 2026). Kept: rating ≥ 8.0, price £–£££, minus obvious fine-dining, tasting-menu and special-occasion-only places — this is a casual-eating map. Bold ring = rated 9.0 or higher.

Stations

Every Underground, Elizabeth line, Overground, DLR and rail station in fare Zones 1–2, from the official TfL API. Bold ring = well-connected: three or more lines, an Elizabeth line stop, or a major interchange.

Good life 🥑

A tongue-in-cheek gentrification index: branches of Waitrose, GAIL's, Aesop and Whole Foods, from their official store finders (Sep 2026). Where these cluster, the flat whites are reliable and the rent is not.

Crime heat

Recorded crime from the Metropolitan Police via the London Datastore (LSOA-level, 12 months to Jul 2026). Each small statistical area gets a severity score:

score = 0.7 × violent crime density + 0.3 × theft density

where violent crime = violence with injury + homicide + personal robbery + residential burglary (threats, harassment and no-injury assault are deliberately excluded — about 74% of the raw "violence" bucket involves no physical harm), and theft = theft from the person + other theft + bicycle theft (no shoplifting). Densities are per km² of each area's true size, each component normalized to its own 95th percentile, and the color ramp saturates at the 99th percentile of the score. Positions are area centroids (ONS), so the surface has ~250 m resolution — read it street-corridor by street-corridor, not door by door.
